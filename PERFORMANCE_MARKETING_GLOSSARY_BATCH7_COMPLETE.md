### ✅ Formula Clarity
All metrics include clear formulas:
- **CAC** = Total Sales & Marketing Costs / New Customers
- **LTV** = ARPA × Gross Margin % × Customer Lifespan
- **LTV:CAC** = LTV / CAC
- **ROAS** = Attributed Revenue / Ad Spend
- **MER** = Total Revenue / Total Marketing Spend
- **CPA** = Total Cost / Acquisitions
- **CPL** = Total Cost / Leads
- **CPM** = (Total Cost / Impressions) × 1,000
- **CPC** = Total Cost / Clicks
- **Payback Period** = CAC / Monthly Gross Profit
- **Incrementality** = (Treatment - Control) / Control
- **Conversion Lift** = (Treatment Rate - Control Rate) / Control Rate
