## 2. Strategic Tools (9 Fully Functional)

### Tool 1: GTM Budget Lab
**Purpose**: Model marketing budget allocation across channels
**Inputs**:
- Monthly budget
- Company stage
- Business model
- Average contract value
- Sales cycle
- Target market
- ICP
- Team size
- Current channels
- GTM motion
- Primary objective

**Outputs**:
- Channel allocation with percentages
- Budget allocation per channel
- Rationale for each allocation
- Testing period recommendation
- Expected learning objectives
- Priority ranking
- Decision framework explanation

**Decision Framework**: Based on ACV thresholds:
- >$100k ACV: Enterprise focus (LinkedIn 30%, Content 25%, Events 20%, Outbound 15%, Partnerships 10%)
- $25k-$100k ACV: Mid-market balance (Content 30%, LinkedIn 25%, Paid Search 20%, Outbound 15%, Webinars 10%)
- <$25k ACV: SMB scalable (Content 35%, Paid Search 25%, Paid Social 20%, LinkedIn 10%, Partnerships 10%)

### Tool 2: B2B Channel Planner
**Purpose**: Evaluate and prioritize B2B marketing channels
**Inputs**:
- ICP
- ACV
- Budget
- Sales cycle
- Current channels
- Goals

**Outputs**:
- Channel recommendations based on ACV
- Reasoning for each recommendation
- Priority levels
- Confidence indicators
- Risks and assumptions
- Next steps

**Channel Evaluation Framework**:
- High ACV (>$50k): High-touch channels (LinkedIn, Outbound, Events)
- Mid ACV ($10k-$50k): Balanced approach (LinkedIn, Content, Paid Search)
- Low ACV (<$10k): Scalable channels (Content, Paid Search, Paid Social)

### Tool 6: Ad Copy Analyzer
**Purpose**: Analyze and score ad copy effectiveness
**Inputs**:
- Ad copy text
- Target audience
- Goal
- Platform

**Outputs**:
- Overall score (0-10)

  - Pain point articulation
  - Value proposition

**Scoring Logic**:
- Hook: First 5 words impact
- Clarity: Length and simplicity

- Value Prop: Benefit language detection
- Specificity: Number/metric presence
- CTA: Action verb detection

## 3. Tool UX Design

### Consistent Flow
Every tool follows the strategic consulting experience:
1. **INPUTS** → Clear, guided data collection
2. **ANALYSIS** → Transparent reasoning
3. **DIAGNOSIS** → Problem identification
4. **RECOMMENDATION** → Clear action items
5. **RATIONALE** → Why this recommendation
6. **ACTION PLAN** → Specific next steps
7. **RELATED TOOLS** → Complementary resources

### Output Sharing
- Copy results to clipboard
- Download as text file
- Share-friendly formatting

### Quality Standards
- Every recommendation is explainable
- No "AI says..." or "Recommended because AI..."
- Explicit reasoning: "Recommended because your ACV, sales cycle, and ICP concentration make this channel economically viable"
- Assumptions clearly stated
- Risks acknowledged
- Confidence levels indicated
