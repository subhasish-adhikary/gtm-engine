## Content Differentiation

### ABM vs Account-Based Marketing
Clearly explained that these are the same concept, with ABM being the abbreviation. The entries complement each other without artificial distinction.

### ABM Lite vs One-to-Few ABM
Clear distinction: ABM Lite uses broader segmentation for 500-5000 accounts, while One-to-Few ABM uses tighter clusters of 5-15 accounts with more personalization.

### One-to-One vs One-to-Few vs Programmatic ABM
Clear progression explained:
- One-to-One: 10-50 accounts, deep personalization
- One-to-Few: 50-500 accounts, cluster-based personalization
- Programmatic: 500+ accounts, technology-driven at scale

### Account Intelligence vs Account Engagement
Clear distinction: Intelligence is the data and insights about accounts; Engagement is the measurement of interactions with those accounts.
