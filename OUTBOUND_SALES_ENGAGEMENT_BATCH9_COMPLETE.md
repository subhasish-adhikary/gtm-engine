## Terms Completed (17 Total)

### Core Outbound Concepts (6 terms)
1. **Cold Email**
   - Definition: Unsolicited business email sent to recipients with no established relationship
   - Legitimate B2B use: Sales prospecting, partnership development, recruiting, business development
   - Practical execution: Targeted prospecting, personalized messaging, compliance with regulations
   - Limitations: Requires discipline, personalization, compliance; can damage reputation if done poorly
   - Compliance: CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL compliance required
   - Related: cold-email-marketing, email-personalization, email-deliverability, email-warmup, domain-reputation, sender-reputation, sales-engagement

2. **Cold Email Marketing**
   - Definition: Structured use of outbound email to identify, reach, and engage potential customers
   - Legitimate B2B use: Scalable prospecting, pipeline generation, reaching prospects not reachable through other channels
   - Practical execution: Building targeted lists, crafting personalized messages, managing sending infrastructure, tracking performance
   - Limitations: Requires significant investment in infrastructure, personalization, compliance
   - Compliance: CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL compliance required
   - Related: cold-email, email-personalization, email-deliverability, email-warmup, domain-reputation, sender-reputation, sales-engagement, multichannel-outbound

3. **Outbound Marketing**
   - Definition: Proactively initiates contact with prospective audiences through interruptive or proactive methods
   - Legitimate B2B use: Reaching prospects not actively searching, targeting specific accounts, generating pipeline quickly
   - Practical execution: Defining target audience, crafting compelling messaging, selecting channels, executing outreach
   - Limitations: Can damage brand reputation if done poorly; requires careful targeting and personalization
   - Compliance: Must respect prospects and comply with regulations
   - Related: inbound-marketing, account-based-marketing, cold-email, cold-calling, account-based-outbound, multichannel-outbound

4. **Account-Based Outbound**
   - Definition: Focuses outbound activity on a defined set of target accounts rather than treating entire market equally
   - Legitimate B2B use: Targeting high-value accounts, increasing reply and meeting rates, complementing ABM
   - Practical execution: Identifying target accounts, researching each account, crafting personalized outreach, coordinating across channels
   - Limitations: Requires significant research and personalization; more resource-intensive than traditional outbound
   - Compliance: Must comply with email and calling regulations
   - Related: account-based-marketing, outbound-marketing, multichannel-outbound, sales-engagement, account-based-sales

5. **Multichannel Outbound**
   - Definition: Coordinates prospecting across multiple channels (email, phone, social, etc.)
   - Legitimate B2B use: Increasing engagement by reaching prospects through preferred channels
   - Practical execution: Coordinating outreach across channels in structured sequence
   - Limitations: Requires coordination across channels; can be annoying if not done properly
   - Compliance: Must comply with regulations for each channel
   - Related: outbound-marketing, sales-engagement, sales-sequencing, multichannel-sequencing, account-based-outbound

6. **Cold Calling**
   - Definition: Contacting prospective customers by telephone without previously established relationship
   - Legitimate B2B use: Direct real-time communication, initiating relationships, complementing other channels
   - Practical execution: Building targeted lists, researching prospects, crafting compelling messaging, handling objections
   - Limitations: Can damage brand reputation if done poorly; requires discipline and respect for prospects
   - Compliance: Must comply with calling regulations (TCPA, etc.)
   - Related: outbound-marketing, sales-engagement, sales-sequencing, multichannel-outbound, cold-email

### Email Infrastructure & Deliverability (4 terms)
7. **Email Deliverability**
   - Definition: Ability of legitimate email to successfully reach recipients' mailboxes
   - Legitimate B2B use: Ensuring cold email reaches inbox, improving campaign effectiveness
   - Practical execution: Warming up domains, limiting sending volume, verifying email addresses, avoiding spam triggers
   - Limitations: Requires ongoing monitoring and maintenance; can be impacted by many factors
   - Compliance: Must comply with email provider guidelines
   - Related: email-warmup, domain-reputation, sender-reputation, cold-email, cold-email-marketing

8. **Email Warmup**
   - Definition: Practices to establish or improve sending reputation of new or inactive email domain/mailbox
   - Legitimate B2B use: Establishing sending reputation for new domains, improving deliverability
   - Practical execution: Gradually increasing sending volume over 2-4 weeks using warmup tools
   - Limitations: Takes time (2-4 weeks); requires warmup tools
   - Compliance: Not to be confused with permission to send unsolicited email
   - Related: email-deliverability, domain-reputation, sender-reputation, cold-email, cold-email-marketing

9. **Domain Reputation**
   - Definition: Reputation associated with internet domain based on factors influencing email evaluation
   - Legitimate B2B use: Ensuring email deliverability, protecting sender reputation
   - Practical execution: Monitoring domain reputation, maintaining good sending practices
   - Limitations: Can be impacted by many factors; requires ongoing monitoring
   - Compliance: Must comply with email provider guidelines
   - Related: email-deliverability, sender-reputation, email-warmup, cold-email, cold-email-marketing

10. **Sender Reputation**
    - Definition: Reputation associated with email sender, domain, IP, or sending infrastructure
    - Legitimate B2B use: Ensuring email deliverability, protecting sender reputation
    - Practical execution: Monitoring sender reputation, maintaining good sending practices, implementing authentication
    - Limitations: Can be impacted by many factors; requires ongoing monitoring
    - Compliance: Must comply with email provider guidelines and authentication requirements
    - Related: email-deliverability, domain-reputation, email-warmup, cold-email, cold-email-marketing

### Personalization & Targeting (3 terms)
11. **Email Personalization**
    - Definition: Adapts outbound message based on information relevant to recipient, account, role, business situation, or signal
    - Legitimate B2B use: Increasing reply rates, demonstrating relevance and research
    - Practical execution: Researching recipients, identifying personalization triggers, crafting personalized messages
    - Limitations: Must be genuine and relevant; superficial personalization is ineffective
    - Compliance: Must comply with email regulations
    - Related: cold-email, cold-email-marketing, account-based-outbound, intent-based-outreach, trigger-based-outreach

12. **Intent-Based Outreach**
    - Definition: Uses signals suggesting company or person may have active business need for outreach timing/messaging
    - Legitimate B2B use: Timing outreach based on intent signals, increasing reply rates
    - Practical execution: Monitoring intent signals, identifying prospects showing intent, reaching out with timely messaging
    - Limitations: Requires intent data; must validate signals before outreach
    - Compliance: Must comply with email and calling regulations
    - Related: trigger-based-outreach, account-based-outbound, intent-data, outbound-marketing, account-based-marketing

13. **Trigger-Based Outreach**
    - Definition: Initiates sales communication after specific business or behavioral event occurs
    - Legitimate B2B use: Timing outreach based on trigger events, increasing reply rates
    - Practical execution: Monitoring trigger events, identifying prospects experiencing triggers, reaching out with timely messaging
    - Limitations: Requires trigger event monitoring; must validate events before outreach
    - Compliance: Must comply with email and calling regulations
    - Related: intent-based-outreach, account-based-outbound, outbound-marketing, account-based-marketing, sales-engagement

### Sequencing & Coordination (2 terms)
14. **Sales Sequencing**
    - Definition: Structured scheduling of multiple sales touchpoints across defined period
    - Legitimate B2B use: Increasing engagement by reaching out multiple times through multiple channels
    - Practical execution: Defining sequence of touches across channels, personalizing each touch, optimizing based on data
    - Limitations: Must be personalized and provide value; can be annoying if not done properly
    - Compliance: Must comply with email and calling regulations
    - Related: multichannel-sequencing, sales-engagement, outbound-marketing, cold-email, cold-calling

15. **Multichannel Sequencing**
    - Definition: Coordinates planned series of sales touches across multiple channels
    - Legitimate B2B use: Increasing engagement by reaching prospects through preferred channels
    - Practical execution: Coordinating sequence of touches across multiple channels, personalizing each touch
    - Limitations: Requires coordination across channels; can be annoying if not done properly
    - Compliance: Must comply with regulations for each channel
    - Related: sales-sequencing, multichannel-outbound, sales-engagement, outbound-marketing, account-based-outbound
