## Editorial Quality Standards

### Research Quality
- All articles cite authoritative sources (Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey, Google, Salesforce)
- Statistics are verified and attributed
- No fabricated data or quotes
- Current research from 2024-2025

### Writing Quality
- No AI slop phrases ("In today's rapidly evolving landscape", "revolutionizing", etc.)
- Natural editorial rhythm with varied sentence structure
- Original analysis and synthesis, not just summarization
- Clear arguments with evidence and examples
- Acknowledgment of limitations and counterarguments

### Structure
Each article follows the editorial framework:
- H1 + Thesis
- Author + Date + Reading time
- At a Glance (key statistics)
- Table of Contents
- Main argument with evidence
- Practical implementation guidance
- FAQ section
- Sources & References
- Related thinking
- Related tools
- Author bio

### E-E-A-T Compliance
- **Experience**: Articles reference real implementation patterns
- **Expertise**: Deep domain knowledge demonstrated throughout
- **Authoritativeness**: Cited sources are industry-leading
- **Trustworthiness**: Clear distinction between fact, interpretation, and opinion
