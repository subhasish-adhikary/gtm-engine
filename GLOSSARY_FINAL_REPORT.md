## Audit Findings Summary

### Issue 1: Duplicate Concepts (9 terms)
**Examples:**
- CAC ↔ Customer Acquisition Cost
- ABM ↔ Account-Based Marketing
- RevOps ↔ Revenue Operations
- CDP ↔ Customer Data Platform
- Marketing Attribution ↔ Attribution

**Action:** Remove duplicates, keep primary versions
**Impact:** High - Creates confusion and dilutes SEO value
**Timeline:** Week 1, Day 1-2

### Issue 2: Near-Duplicates (5 pairs, 10 terms)
**Examples:**
- Category Design ↔ Category Creation
- GTM Operations ↔ GTM Systems
- Content Operations ↔ Content Intelligence
- Sales Operations ↔ Funnel Operations ↔ Pipeline Operations
- AI-Assisted Marketing ↔ AI-Augmented Marketing

**Action:** Merge into single pages with clear differentiation
**Impact:** Medium - Creates keyword cannibalization
**Timeline:** Week 2, Day 1-2

### Issue 3: Abbreviation Pages (12 terms)
**Examples:**
- PLG → Product-Led Growth
- SLG → Sales-Led Growth
- ICP → Ideal Customer Profile
- CDP → Customer Data Platform
- ABM → Account-Based Marketing

**Action:** Convert to redirects to full terms
**Impact:** Medium - Creates thin pages
**Timeline:** Week 1, Day 2-3

### Issue 4: Thin Pages (47 terms)
**Examples:**
- gtm-intelligence, abm-lite, programmatic-abm
- one-to-one-abm, one-to-few-abm, account-intelligence
- ai-native-marketing, ai-assisted-marketing, ai-augmented-marketing
- content-led-growth, content-distribution, content-repurposing

**Action:** Expand with more depth, examples, citations
**Impact:** High - Poor user experience and SEO
**Timeline:** Week 2-3

### Issue 5: Incorrect Categories (14 terms)
**Examples:**
- product-led-growth, sales-led-growth, founder-led-growth
- community-led-growth, partner-led-growth, ecosystem-led-growth
- founder-led-sales, founder-led-marketing, ecosystem-marketing

**Action:** Recategorize or create new categories
**Impact:** Medium - Poor organization
**Timeline:** Week 2, Day 3-4

### Issue 6: Inconsistent Definitions (4 pairs)
**Examples:**
- GTM Strategy vs GTM Motion
- Marketing Attribution vs Multi-Touch Attribution
- Content Marketing vs Content-Led Growth
- AI-Assisted Marketing vs AI-Augmented Marketing

**Action:** Clarify or merge definitions
**Impact:** Medium - Creates confusion
**Timeline:** Week 1, Day 3-4

### Issue 7: Missing Relationships (50+ relationships)
**Examples:**
- CAC should link to LTV, LTV:CAC, Payback Period, ROAS
- LTV should link to CAC, LTV:CAC, Payback Period, Retention
- Attribution should link to Multi-Touch, First-Touch, Last-Touch, Incrementality
- ICP should link to Persona, Buyer Persona, Account Selection, Account Tiering

**Action:** Add bidirectional relatedTerms links
**Impact:** High - Weak knowledge graph
**Timeline:** Week 1, Day 4-5 and Week 3, Day 3-4

### Issue 8: Keyword Cannibalization (5 sets)
**Examples:**
- GTM Strategy vs GTM Motion vs GTM Engineering
- Content Marketing vs Content-Led Growth vs Content Operations
- AI Marketing vs AI Agents vs Agentic Marketing
- Paid Search vs Search Advertising
- Retargeting vs Remarketing

**Action:** Consolidate or clearly differentiate
**Impact:** Medium - SEO competition
**Timeline:** Week 2-3

### Issue 9: Missing Citations (10+ claims)
**Examples:**
- "Companies with mature GTM engineering see 2-3x faster revenue growth"
- "PLG companies grow 2-3x faster than traditional SaaS"
- "ABM accounts close 27% faster"
- "Marketing automation increases revenue by 14.5%"

**Action:** Add citations or remove claims
**Impact:** Medium - Credibility issues
**Timeline:** Week 2, Day 4-5

### Issue 10: AI-Sounding Definitions (4 terms)
**Examples:**
- gtm-intelligence: "GTM intelligence is the systematic collection..."
- ai-native-marketing: "AI-native marketing represents a fundamental shift..."
- ai-assisted-marketing: "AI-assisted marketing leverages artificial intelligence..."
- content-intelligence: "Content intelligence combines market, customer..."

**Action:** Rewrite with concrete language
**Impact:** Low - Quality issues
**Timeline:** Week 3, Day 4-5
