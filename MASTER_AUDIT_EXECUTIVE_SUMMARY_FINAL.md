## FINAL RULE

### The goal is NOT to make the website "look impressive."

### The goal is to create a genuinely differentiated professional GTM / Growth Marketing knowledge and tools platform that demonstrates expertise through:

- original thinking
- useful tools
- strategic frameworks
- practical implementation knowledge
- high-quality content
- strong information architecture
- credible evidence
- excellent UX
- technical quality
- machine-readable knowledge

### If a competitor has a feature that does not add meaningful value, do not copy it.

### If the current website already does something better, preserve it.

### If you identify a genuinely superior approach, explain the reasoning before implementing it.

### Do not generate generic AI recommendations.

### Do not fabricate data.

### Do not fabricate benchmarks.

### Do not fabricate citations.

### Do not fabricate professional experience.

### Prioritize depth, usefulness, credibility and differentiation over feature count.
