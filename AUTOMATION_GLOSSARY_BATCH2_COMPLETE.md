### ✅ Clear Concept Differentiation
Successfully differentiated between commonly confused concepts:

**Automation vs. Orchestration vs. Operations:**
- **Marketing Automation**: Technology category (tools like HubSpot, Marketo)
- **Workflow Automation**: Specific automated processes within marketing automation
- **Marketing Orchestration**: Multi-channel coordination (broader than automation)
- **Marketing Operations**: Function/team that manages all this
- **Revenue Operations**: Cross-functional alignment beyond just marketing

**Lead Scoring vs. Predictive Lead Scoring:**
- **Lead Scoring**: Rules-based numerical values
- **Predictive Lead Scoring**: ML-based scoring using historical data

**Trigger-Based vs. Event-Based vs. Behavioral:**
- **Trigger-Based Marketing**: Simple event-response (form submitted → send email)
- **Event-Based Marketing**: Strategic signals (funding raised → personalized outreach)
- **Behavioral Automation**: Pattern-driven (consumption patterns → personalized content)

**Qualification vs. Scoring vs. Routing:**
- **Lead Scoring**: Continuous numerical scale
- **Automated Qualification**: Binary decision (qualified or not)
- **Lead Routing**: Assignment to sales rep/territory
