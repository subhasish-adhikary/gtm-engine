# Glossary Audit & Consolidation - Complete Summary

## Executive Summary

Successfully completed comprehensive audit of 222 glossary terms and created detailed implementation plan for consolidation to ~150 authoritative terms.

## Audit Findings

### Critical Issues Identified: 47

1. **Duplicate Concepts (9 terms)**
   - CAC ↔ Customer Acquisition Cost
   - ABM ↔ Account-Based Marketing
   - RevOps ↔ Revenue Operations
   - CDP ↔ Customer Data Platform
   - Marketing Attribution ↔ Attribution
   - Retargeting ↔ Remarketing
   - Marketing Mix Modeling ↔ Media Mix Modeling
   - AI Marketing (duplicate entry)
   - AI Agents (duplicate entry)

2. **Near-Duplicates (5 pairs, 10 terms)**
   - Category Design ↔ Category Creation
   - GTM Operations ↔ GTM Systems
   - Content Operations ↔ Content Intelligence
   - Sales Operations ↔ Funnel Operations ↔ Pipeline Operations
   - AI-Assisted Marketing ↔ AI-Augmented Marketing

3. **Abbreviation Pages (12 terms)**
   - PLG, SLG, ICP, CDP, ABM, RevOps, CAC, AEO, GEO, LLMO, RAG
   - Should be redirects to full terms

4. **Thin Pages (47 terms)**
   - Pages with insufficient depth, examples, or citations
   - Need expansion to 200-300 words with examples and citations

5. **Incorrect Categories (14 terms)**
   - Terms in wrong categories
   - Need recategorization or new category creation

6. **Inconsistent Definitions (4 pairs)**
   - Overlapping definitions that need clarification
   - Need clear differentiation or merging

7. **Missing Relationships (50+ relationships)**
   - Missing relatedTerms relationships
   - Need to add bidirectional links

8. **Keyword Cannibalization (5 sets)**
   - Terms competing for same keywords
   - Need clear differentiation or consolidation

9. **Missing Citations (10+ claims)**
   - Claims without citations
   - Need to add authoritative citations or remove claims

10. **AI-Sounding Definitions (4 terms)**
    - Definitions that sound AI-generated
    - Need rewriting with more concrete language
