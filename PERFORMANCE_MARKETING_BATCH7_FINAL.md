### Customer Economics Metrics (5 terms)
4. **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** (Featured)
   - Formula: CAC = Total Sales & Marketing Costs / New Customers
   - Numerator: All acquisition costs (salaries, ads, tools, events, content)
   - Denominator: Number of new customers acquired
   - **Explicitly explains CAC = Customer Acquisition Cost (synonymous)**
   - Practical B2B SaaS example: $300K costs / 50 customers = $6,000 CAC

5. **Customer Acquisition Cost**
   - Formula: Customer Acquisition Cost = Total Sales & Marketing Costs / New Customers
   - **Explicitly explains this is the same as CAC**
   - Practical B2B SaaS example included

6. **LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)** (Featured)
   - Formula: LTV = ARPA × Gross Margin % × Customer Lifespan
   - Numerator: Average revenue per account × gross margin
   - Denominator: Customer lifespan (months/years)
   - Practical B2B SaaS example: $1,000 × 80% × 36 = $28,800 LTV

7. **LTV:CAC Ratio**
   - Formula: LTV:CAC = LTV / CAC
   - Numerator: Customer lifetime value
   - Denominator: Customer acquisition cost
   - Practical B2B SaaS example: $28,800 / $6,000 = 4.8:1 ratio

8. **Payback Period**
   - Formula: Payback Period = CAC / Monthly Gross Profit
   - Numerator: Customer acquisition cost
   - Denominator: Monthly gross profit per customer
   - Practical B2B SaaS example: $6,000 / $800 = 7.5 months

### Advertising Cost Metrics (5 terms)
9. **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**
   - Formula: ROAS = Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend
   - Numerator: Revenue from customers acquired through advertising
   - Denominator: Total advertising spend
   - Practical B2B SaaS example: $250K revenue / $50K spend = 5:1 ROAS

10. **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**
    - Formula: CPA = Total Cost / Acquisitions
    - Numerator: Total campaign/channel costs
    - Denominator: Number of acquisitions/conversions
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: $10K / 50 = $200 CPA

11. **CPL (Cost Per Lead)**
    - Formula: CPL = Total Cost / Leads
    - Numerator: Total campaign costs
    - Denominator: Number of leads generated
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: $5K / 100 leads = $50 CPL

12. **CPM (Cost Per Mille)**
    - Formula: CPM = (Total Cost / Impressions) × 1,000
    - Numerator: Total advertising cost
    - Denominator: Number of impressions (× 1,000)
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: ($10K / 500K) × 1,000 = $20 CPM

13. **CPC (Cost Per Click)**
    - Formula: CPC = Total Cost / Clicks
    - Numerator: Total advertising cost
    - Denominator: Number of clicks
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: $5K / 500 clicks = $10 CPC

### Advanced Measurement Concepts (5 terms)
14. **MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)**
    - Formula: MER = Total Revenue / Total Marketing Spend
    - Numerator: Total revenue
    - Denominator: Total marketing spend (all costs)
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: $2M revenue / $400K spend = 5:1 MER

15. **Incrementality** (Emerging)
    - Formula: Incrementality = (Treatment - Control) / Control
    - Numerator: Difference in outcomes (with marketing - without marketing)
    - Denominator: Outcomes without marketing (control group)
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: 20% lift from content marketing

16. **Marketing Mix Modeling**
    - Statistical analysis method (no simple formula)
    - Uses regression analysis on aggregate data
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: Content marketing 5:1 ROI, events 3:1, Google Ads 2:1

17. **Media Mix Modeling**
    - **Explicitly explains this is interchangeable with Marketing Mix Modeling**
    - Statistical analysis method (no simple formula)
    - Practical B2B SaaS example included

18. **Conversion Lift** (Emerging)
    - Formula: Conversion Lift = (Treatment Rate - Control Rate) / Control Rate
    - Numerator: Difference in conversion rates
    - Denominator: Control group conversion rate
    - Practical B2B SaaS example: (12% - 10%) / 10% = 20% lift
